
## Local house-rule additions (this project, beyond upstream STE)

This project adds two rules on top of the ASD-STE100 ruleset above. Official
STE does not require either one. Apply both in Strict mode and in
STE-flavored mode.

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| No em dashes | Split into two sentences, or use a comma or period. | Any em dash. The "No semicolons" row above notes that official STE permits the em dash. This project bans it anyway. |
| No cataphoric teasers | State the point in the first sentence. | "Here's the thing", "But there's a catch", or any phrase that promises a point instead of making it. |

Scan Checklist item 7: **cataphoric teasers** — a phrase that promises a
point instead of stating it. Fix: delete the teaser, open with the point.
